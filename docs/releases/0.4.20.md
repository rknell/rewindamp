# Rewindamp 0.4.20

Sign in to Rewindamp Podcasts on Android and Linux with a username and password. Each
account has its own subscriptions, download rules, playback positions, played status,
and private device storage. People who subscribe to the same RSS feed share one server
archive download per episode.

On an upgraded podcast server, sign in as `owner` with your existing password. Your
subscriptions and listening history carry over. Open **Users** in the podcast web
console to create, reset, disable, or re-enable listener accounts. Select a show in
either client and open podcast settings to unsubscribe.

Verified the isolated server migration, account isolation, shared audio, and web user
management. Android passed build and lint. Linux passed the disposable Jellyfin and podcast
integration checks.

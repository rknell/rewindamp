# Rewindamp 0.4.12

- Reopening the app restores the last selected Music, Podcasts or Queue destination.
- Marking podcast episodes played or dismissed removes their row in place, keeping your scroll position and loaded pages. Listening history continues syncing in the background.

Validation: JDK 17 / Android SDK 35 debug build and lint passed locally. The release workflow gates publication on isolated Android 15 car/podcast checks, including launcher restoration and marking an episode in a deeply scrolled list. The local emulator crashed during the full podcast suite.

# Rewindamp 0.4.10

- The horizontal destination wheel highlights each new selection in green and requests
  a haptic tick as you turn through its detents. Release to open the selected destination.
  Cancelling restores the starting selection; taps and accessibility actions still work.
- The vertical letter wheel shows more surrounding letters as its panel grows. Labels
  fill the available drum height while the selected letter stays centered and readable.
- Albums hides Singles, missing/unknown-album groups and explicitly labelled single
  releases. Normal album names such as “Singles Going Steady” remain visible. Individual
  songs remain available in Tracks and Artists. The same filtering applies to saved
  album lists and background refreshes.
- Removed Podcasts from the Music Library popup. Use the bottom destination wheel instead.

Validation: JDK 17 debug build and Android lint. Five focused Android 15 emulator checks
cover live highlighted-label rendering, per-detent Android haptic requests, cancellation,
side taps, expanded vertical labels, cached and refreshed album filtering, the music-only
popup, and Queue/Podcasts navigation with saved positions through activity recreation.
Tests used synthetic local metadata; no production services or personal media were used.
Physical haptic strength follows the phone's settings and hardware.

The application ID and signing certificate are unchanged. Update through the existing
Obtainium source: https://github.com/rknell/rewindamp.

# Rewindamp 0.4.23

Linux adds Space to play and pause, Left and Right to seek, Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right to skip tracks or episodes, Ctrl+Minus and Ctrl+Equals to adjust volume, and Ctrl+F to focus music search. Music seeks by five seconds and podcasts use the existing back 15 and forward 30 second intervals. Search fields and focused controls keep their native keyboard behavior.

The Linux now-playing cover is larger and sits to the left of the track title and metadata.
Linux uses the Android launcher artwork for its app menu, window and tray icons. Mixarr sign-in failures show a dialog with guidance based on the cause.

Validated the Linux AppImage in an isolated desktop session, including playback keys, seek distances, keyboard focus, the now-playing layout and the bundled icon. The isolated Jellyfin, podcast and Mixarr checks passed, as did the Android debug build and lint.
